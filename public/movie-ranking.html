The Best Movies According to Asher

My Top 3 Movies of All Time

Iron Man

This movie is one of my all time favorites. I love it because Tony isn't given any crazy super powers or out of this world powers. He uses his mind and upbringing to create the Iron Man suit that initially saves him and then is used to save the world.

Transformers

This movie is a classic for me because it calls upon my childhood watching all the animated Transformer shows. I love it because it stays pretty original to the old shows but bring it to a more live action type of movie. Back when it was released, the CGI was amazing!

The Greatest Showman

I love this movie because initially I hated musicals, but after the movie concluded I was so impressed with the emotions it brought out of me and just the great acting and songs, I was hooked. I haven't watched too many musicals since then, but this is definitely an all time favorite of mine.
